Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21
Plain-language notice: this page describes, honestly and specifically, what ARIA actually does with your data based on how it's built. It is a starting point for transparency with your users, not a substitute for review by a qualified lawyer before this application is used commercially or at scale - especially given it can connect to real third-party accounts (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Chat, Outlook, Outlook Chat, GitHub, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Azure DevOps).

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers anyone who creates an account, signs in as a guest, or uses ARIA ("the app", "we", "our") to chat with uploaded documents, scraped pages, API data, or content pulled from a connected source system.

2. Account information

Depending on how you sign in, we hold:

  • Registered accounts: your name and email address, managed via Supabase Auth. Your password is never stored by this app in plain text - Supabase handles password hashing and storage.
  • Google / Microsoft sign-in: your name, email, and profile picture as provided by Google or Microsoft, linked to your account by Supabase. We never see or store your Google/Microsoft password.
  • Guest accounts: just the name and email you type in - no password. Guest sessions expire automatically after 24 hours.

3. Content you upload or connect

Files you upload, URLs you paste, API responses you fetch, and files/records you pull in from a connected source are parsed, broken into chunks, and converted into vector embeddings so ARIA can answer questions about them. This processed content is stored in a vector database scoped to your session. To generate embeddings and chat answers, the relevant chunks are sent to our configured AI providers (for example Groq, OpenAI, HuggingFace, Voyage AI, and Cohere, depending on current configuration) solely to produce that response - not for their own unrelated use beyond their standard provider terms.

4. Connecting third-party source systems

ARIA can optionally connect to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Chat, Outlook, Outlook Chat, GitHub, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, and Azure DevOps so you can pull a file, email, or chat in and chat with it. If you use this:

  • For accounts you authorize via OAuth (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Chat, Outlook, Outlook Chat, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams), we store an access/refresh token tied specifically to your user account - never shared with other users of this app. Gmail+Google Chat share one Google sign-in, and Outlook+Outlook Chat+SharePoint+Teams share one Microsoft sign-in - connecting or disconnecting any one of a group does all of them together.
  • We only fetch what you explicitly browse and select in the app - we do not run background jobs that read your entire mailbox, drive, or repositories without your action.
  • This feature sits behind an additional access key, separate from your normal login, specifically because it grants access to real external accounts.
  • Disconnecting a source (where available) removes our stored token for it; revoking access on the provider's own side (e.g. Google Account → Security → Third-party access) is the complete way to fully cut off access.

5. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not share your uploaded content or connected-account data with other users.
  • We do not use your content to train third-party models beyond what each configured AI provider's own standard API terms specify for processing a single request.

6. Local storage & cookies

Your login token, active chat session reference, and a few interface preferences (like dark mode) are kept in your browser's local storage, not cookies. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Data retention & deletion

Guest accounts and their data expire automatically after 24 hours. Registered-account data persists until you delete your account or request deletion. To request deletion of your account or connected-source tokens, contact the operator of this deployment directly.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Source-system connectors require a separate access key on top of normal login. Per-user tokens are isolated in the database by user ID, not shared files. No system is perfectly secure, and this description reflects the application's design intent, not a guarantee.

9. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the person or organization operating this deployment of ARIA.
